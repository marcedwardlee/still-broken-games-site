Boilerplate

Copy freely. The short version is one sentence; the long version is one paragraph.

One line

GOAT Knight is a live multiplayer Roblox game about riding an enormous war goat into terrain-driven battlefield chaos — and making it home with a story.

One paragraph

A thousand years ago, a plague took every horse on the steppe. The people who had goats bred them bigger, and bigger, until the cavalry had horns. GOAT Knight is a live multiplayer Roblox game set in what happened next: two warbands, one contested river crossing, and a great deal of animal that does not steer like a vehicle. Between battles, riders return to their ger — where their herd, their companion and their history live. It is made by Still Broken Games, an independent studio in Georgia, USA.

About the studio

Still Broken Games LLC is an independent game studio in Georgia, USA. It ships a live game to real players, tests ideas in public early, and keeps the ones that create good stories. GOAT Knight is its current title.

If you only have three lines

  • The premise is a supply problem, not a joke. Horses went extinct; the goats got bigger. The game opens by telling you so.
  • The ground decides more than the stats do. Momentum, slope, collision and recovery are the mechanics — not a combo tree.
  • The goat is not equipment. The same rider, war goat and companion persist through battle, travel and home.
