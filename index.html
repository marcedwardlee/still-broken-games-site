Still Broken Games LLC

Small studio. Physical games. Memorable messes.

Still Broken Games LLC is an independent game-development studio organized in Georgia. Our current focus is making the first GOAT Knight battlefield readable, replayable, funny, and emotionally strong before expanding the world around it.

We prototype aggressively, keep what survives play, and prefer physical consequence and player-made stories over systems built only to look impressive on a feature list.
